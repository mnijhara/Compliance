# Landing page UX hierarchy

The public homepage follows a deliberate top-to-bottom conversion flow:

1. **Hero** — clear product promise and two primary actions: upload documents or chat with Nova.
2. **Document review** — upload existing HR documents without a setup questionnaire.
3. **How it works** — Read → Review → Act.
4. **Deeper workspace** — Control Center and Advanced Review for users who need establishment context, workflows and audit trails.

The landing page must not lead with the upload form. The hero establishes the product before the first operational interaction. Demo numbers and examples must remain explicitly illustrative and must never be presented as real compliance findings.
